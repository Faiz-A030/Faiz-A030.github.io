About Us

House Of Smith. a contemporary fashion brand rooted in Bandung, Indonesia, established with a vision to redefine everyday style through thoughtful design and refined simplicity. Since its founding in 2010, the brand has embraced the philosophy that clothing should seamlessly accompany daily life—balancing comfort, versatility, and understated elegance.

More than just clothing, House Of Smith represents a way of living—where style becomes an effortless expression of identity, worn with confidence every day.
